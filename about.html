About US Landmarks History

US Landmarks History is a cinematic history-travel series exploring the monuments Americans hold most dear — the stories behind their construction, the people who fought to build them, and what it's actually like to stand in front of them today.

This blog is the written companion to our YouTube Shorts documentary series, sharing the same correspondents and the same commitment to fact-checked, Wikipedia-sourced storytelling.

Our Approach

Every article is built from verified Wikipedia sources — no invented quotes, no exaggerated claims. We combine history with a genuine traveler's perspective: the best time to visit, the best photo spots, and the small details a typical guidebook skips.
